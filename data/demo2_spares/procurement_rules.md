# Seaview Shipping Ltd - Spare Parts Procurement Rules

## Approval limits
- Orders up to USD 2,000: approved by the purchasing officer.
- Orders above USD 2,000 and up to USD 10,000: require approval from the technical superintendent.
- Orders above USD 10,000: require approval from the fleet manager.

## Quotations
For any order above USD 5,000, at least two supplier quotations must be obtained and attached to the purchase request.

## Critical spares
Parts in the Main engine, Steering and Safety categories are critical spares. Each vessel must keep at least one of every critical spare on board, and a used critical spare must be reordered within 48 hours.

## Urgent orders
Urgent delivery charges may be used only when a vessel would otherwise be delayed. The reason must be recorded on the purchase request.
