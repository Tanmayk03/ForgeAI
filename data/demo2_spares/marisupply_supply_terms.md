# Marisupply Ltd - Supply Terms for Seaview Shipping Ltd (2026)

## Ordering
Orders are placed by purchase order sent to po@marisupply.example. Each order line must meet the minimum order quantity listed in the catalogue.

## Prices and discounts
Prices are in US dollars, ex works Singapore. Marisupply gives a 3 percent discount on orders above USD 10,000. There is no other volume discount.

## Delivery
Lead times in the catalogue start from receipt of the purchase order. Air freight to any Indian port costs USD 400 per order; sea freight costs USD 150 per order but adds 10 days to the lead time.

## Payment
Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Late payments incur interest of 1.5 percent per month.

## Warranty
Engine parts carry an 18-month warranty from delivery. Electrical parts carry a 12-month warranty.
