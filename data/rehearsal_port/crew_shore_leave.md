# Port of Kochi - Crew Shore Leave (fictional training document)

## Shore passes
Crew members may go ashore with a shore pass issued by the immigration office at the port gate. The ship agent must submit the crew list to immigration at least 24 hours before arrival. Shore passes are valid only within the city limits of Kochi.

## Hours
Shore leave is allowed between 06:00 and 23:00. Crew must be back on board before 23:00; late return must be reported to the Harbour Master by the master of the vessel.

## Restrictions
Crew of vessels in quarantine, or of vessels without a valid crew list, are not allowed ashore. Crew may not carry more than 2 litres of alcohol or 200 cigarettes when returning on board.

## Seafarers' centre
The Kochi Seafarers' Centre near Gate 2 is open daily from 09:00 to 21:00 and offers free Wi-Fi, a small shop and transport to the city centre every hour.
