# Travel Policy - Shore Staff

## Business travel claims
Employees travelling on approved company business may claim economy-class airfare, hotel accommodation up to INR 6,000 per night, and a daily meal allowance of INR 1,200.

## Submitting a claim
Claims must be submitted in the HR portal within 30 days of returning, with original receipts attached. Claims submitted after 30 days require written approval from the department head.

## Travel during leave
Travel undertaken during approved annual leave is personal and cannot be claimed, unless the employee is formally recalled to work by their manager.
