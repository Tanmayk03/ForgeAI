# Seaview Shipping Ltd - Code of Conduct (Shore Staff)

## Working hours
Standard office hours are 09:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Flexible start times between 08:00 and 10:00 are allowed with line manager agreement.

## Remote work
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week after completing 6 months of service, if their role allows it and their manager agrees.

## Gifts and hospitality
Gifts from suppliers or customers worth more than INR 5,000 must be declared to the compliance team within 7 days. Cash gifts must never be accepted.

## Conflicts of interest
Employees must declare any personal or financial interest in a supplier, customer or competitor to the compliance team before taking part in related decisions.

## Reporting concerns
Concerns about misconduct can be reported to the line manager, to HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

## Dress code
Business casual dress is expected in the office. Safety shoes and helmets are mandatory when visiting vessels or the port.
